Bright-star positional reference: HYG Database v4.1 / Astronexus.
Coordinates are represented as J2000 right ascension / declination and projected
for Santiago de Chile at runtime using local sidereal time.

HYG Database:
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

The lunar transit is an artistic accelerated animation. It intentionally keeps
the Moon full and does not simulate the current lunar phase or actual lunar position.
